    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough, about 200 hours by PowerPyx's estimate (difficulty 4/10), and there are no online trophies. The story takes 60–80 hours; the long tail is the 100% completion checklist. Work in three stages, and keep cheats off — using one disables trophies and saving.

    How the 55 split across the roadmap

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy is flagged as potentially missable by the guides that have played the game. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story — there is no mission replay, but the story trophies pop automatically when you finish their mission, and every side questline stays open after the credits.

    One-time moments — do them when they appear

    These trophies are tied to a single spot, vehicle or event rather than a grind. None is confirmed missable, but there is no reason to put them off: grab each one the first time the chance comes up.

    Save-slot safety net

    There is no mission replay and no New Game+, but there are 15 manual save slots and one autosave. PowerPyx recommends keeping a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves) and rotating the other five for free-roam cleanup — cheap insurance if anything ever goes wrong.

    Sources & method

    Every guide in this ledger is written in our own words for this entry. It may draw on other guides and cites them — a source is cited, never copied. The official trophy names and descriptions are the exception: they are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them.

    How we decided what is missable

    Missable flags come from two places: the official descriptions (a trophy tied to one named mission or one-off moment) and PowerPyx's launch guide, which played the game and flags exactly one trophy — Positive Mental Attitude — as potentially missable. Story trophies are automatic on mission completion and cannot be missed; the side-character questlines all stay available after the story. Where PowerPyx marks a trophy as not yet known, the guide says so plainly and works from the official description alone instead of inventing a method.
